Where we send things

The United States only: all fifty states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico. Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico pay the same flat rate and hit the free-shipping threshold on the same terms, but ground transit takes a good deal longer and your confirmation email will say so.

PO boxes and APO, FPO and DPO military addresses are fine, by United States Postal Service, with two exceptions on size. The Suitcase Record Player and the Black Gallery Frame Set exceed the limits for those services and need a street address. Order one to a PO box and we will telephone you rather than cancelling behind your back.

No international shipping, no freight forwarders, and a US billing card does not change either.

What it costs

$8.95 flat per order, whatever is in it and wherever it is going. Free over $65 before tax. Expedited is a flat $18.95 per order with no free threshold.

No handling fee, no packaging fee, no fuel surcharge, no minimum order, and — the one worth naming — no oversize fee on the record player, which costs us roughly three times the flat rate to send. We carry that rather than springing it on you at the last step.

One order, one charge
An order that travels as two parcels still pays one shipping charge, and you may get two tracking numbers.

Two clocks, kept separate

### How long we take to pack - Cards, mugs, kitchen timer, alarm clock: 1 business day - Radio, dominoes, chess, checkers, percolator, bowl, wall clock, frame sets: 1 to 2 business days - Enamel Milk Can: 2 business days, packed in a moulded insert - Suitcase Record Player: 2 to 3 business days, double-boxed - Anything ordered after 1:00pm Mountain Standard Time, at a weekend, or on a federal holiday starts its clock on the next business day

A note on our time zone: Arizona does not observe daylight saving. From March to November we are an hour behind the rest of Mountain Time and level with the Pacific coast. Bear that in mind if you are telephoning.

How long the carrier takes

Their estimates, not guarantees, and not including our packing time.

  • Ground, lower forty-eight: 3 to 6 business days
  • Ground, Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico: 6 to 12 business days
  • Expedited, lower forty-eight only: 1 to 3 business days
  • USPS to PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO: 4 to 10 business days, and military addresses can take substantially longer once inside the military postal system, which no carrier tracks

Added together, a standard order to a street address in the lower forty-eight usually lands four to eight business days after you place it.

Carriers and tracking

USPS Ground Advantage, USPS Priority Mail, UPS Ground and UPS 2nd Day Air, picked on size, weight and destination. There is no carrier choice at checkout, because for most of our parcels it would change neither the price nor the day it arrives.

You get two emails: order confirmation, then shipping confirmation with the carrier and tracking number when it leaves us. Tracking usually starts moving within a day; no overnight update is normal.

Four business days with no shipping confirmation means something has gone wrong here. Telephone (520) 555-0183 or email howdy@sundaydrivegoods.com and we will find out what and say so plainly.

When we are going to be late

The Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule sets out what a seller has to do about a late order. Rather than leave it implied, here it is.

  • We advertise only shipping times we have a reasonable basis to meet. Where we state no time, we ship within 30 days of receiving a properly completed order.
  • If we will miss the date we gave you, we contact you on or before that date, with either a definite revised date or a plain statement that we cannot give one, plus your right to cancel for a full refund at no cost to you.
  • If the revised date is 30 days or less beyond the original, you need not reply: silence counts as agreement to the short delay, and you can still cancel any time before it ships.
  • If the revised date is more than 30 days beyond, or if we cannot give one, the order is cancelled and refunded automatically unless you tell us expressly that you would rather wait.
  • If we miss the revised date too, we write again beforehand, and silence is no longer agreement — without your express consent we cancel and refund.
  • Cancelling takes one line: reply to the notice, telephone (520) 555-0183, or email howdy@sundaydrivegoods.com. We will not ask why.

A cancellation for delay is refunded to the original payment method in full within 7 working days.

Damage, loss, and the enamel question

### Damaged on arrival Email howdy@sundaydrivegoods.com within 14 days of delivery with the order number and photographs of the item and the outer carton. We replace where we have stock, refund in full where we do not, and pay the postage either way. Keep the packaging until we reply.

Enamelware deserves a specific note. A piece that arrives chipped is transit damage and we replace it, no argument. A piece that chips later, because it met a tile floor, is the material behaving the way enamel behaves and is not a fault — it is written on every enamel product page for exactly this reason.

Glass in the frame sets

The Black Gallery Frame Set is glazed with real glass. It is packed with corner protection and it almost always arrives intact, but if any pane is cracked, photograph it in the box before you unpack further and we will replace the whole set.

Marked delivered, nothing there

Check with everyone at the address and the usual spots first — most turn up inside a day. Still nothing after three business days, tell us. We open the carrier claim and, whatever the carrier decides, you get a replacement or a refund. Chasing carriers is our job, not yours.

Tracking has stopped

Nothing moving for seven consecutive business days: tell us and we open a lost-in-transit claim. Replacement or refund, whichever you prefer.

We sent the wrong thing

Our mistake, prepaid label, correct item out at once, and you are not out of pocket for anything.

Addresses

Check the delivery address before ordering. A parcel returned as undeliverable because the address was wrong or incomplete is refunded in full less the actual return-transit cost the carrier charged us, shown to you on their invoice. If the error is ours, we send it again at our cost.

Once a parcel has shipped we cannot change the address. Spot it before it goes and telephone us.

Sales tax

Calculated at checkout from your delivery address where applicable, shown as its own line before you confirm, never folded into the goods or shipping price. As an Arizona business we collect Arizona transaction privilege tax where due, and we collect in other states where economic-nexus thresholds require it.
