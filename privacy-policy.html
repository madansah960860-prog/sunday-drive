What this covers

How Sunday Drive Mercantile LLC, trading as Sunday Drive Goods, handles personal information collected through sundaydrivegoods.com, our order emails, the dispatch mailing list, and telephone orders on (520) 555-0183.

We sell household goods. We are not in the advertising business, and this site loads no analytics tag, no advertising pixel, no social embed and no third-party script of any kind. You can confirm that by viewing the page source; it is a fact about the code, not a promise about our intentions.

What we collect

### What you hand over Order information :: Name, delivery address, billing address, email address, telephone number. Needed to pack a parcel, address it, and tell you it has gone. Payment information :: Entered on our payment processor's own hosted page. Your full card number never reaches our servers and is never stored by us. We receive the last four digits, the card brand, the authorisation result and the amount. Messages :: Your name, email address, an order number if you give one, and whatever you wrote. Dispatch mailing list :: Your email address and the date you asked to join. Telephone orders :: The same order information, written down and then entered in the order system.

What is collected without you doing anything

Our host keeps ordinary server logs — requesting IP address, timestamp, file requested, HTTP status, referring page, browser user-agent. They exist for security and diagnostics, are kept 30 days, and are then deleted.

The site sets no cookies, first-party or third-party. Your cart is held in your browser's localStorage, a browser storage feature rather than a cookie: it stays on your machine, is never transmitted to us, and clearing your browser data removes it. We cannot see what is in anybody's cart.

What we do with it

- Pack, address and send your order, and deal with returns and refunds. - Send transactional email: order confirmation, shipping confirmation with tracking, a delay notice if one becomes necessary, and refund confirmations. - Answer your question, or telephone you back. - Send the dispatch, if you asked for it and until you ask us to stop. - Keep the records tax and accounting law require. - Spot and prevent fraudulent orders.

We build no advertising profiles, do no profiling, and make no automated decisions about anybody.

Cookies

You will not be asked to accept cookies on this site, for the simple reason that it sets none. There is nothing here following you around.

Two browser features are in use and both live on your own device: localStorage for the cart, and your browser's ordinary HTTP cache for our stylesheet and images.

Global Privacy Control signals and Do Not Track headers are honoured by default, in the plain sense that there is no cross-context tracking here for them to disable.

Who else gets it

Only the service providers it actually takes to send a parcel. Each is engaged as a service provider or processor and may use the information only for that job, never for its own purposes.

Payment processor
Name, billing address, email and card details, taken directly from you on its own hosted page, to take payment and issue refunds.
Carriers, USPS and UPS
Name, delivery address, telephone number and parcel weight, to deliver and provide tracking.
Email provider
Email address and the content of transactional email and the dispatch.
Web host
The site files and the 30-day server logs above.
Accountant and tax authorities
Order and payment records, as the law requires.

We also disclose where legally compelled — a valid subpoena, court order or lawful demand — and we will tell you where we are permitted to. If the business were sold, customer records could pass to the buyer as part of it, and we would email everybody affected before it happened.

This business has never sold personal information, and it does not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. No exceptions by age, and none for anyone under 16.

Rights, honoured for everybody

Several states grant privacy rights to their own residents. We honour all of these for every customer rather than checking your address first.

  • To know — the categories and specific pieces we hold, their sources, why we have them, and who we have disclosed them to.
  • To delete — subject only to records tax, accounting and fraud-prevention law requires us to keep.
  • To correct — anything inaccurate.
  • To opt out of sale or sharing — there is nothing to opt out of, because we do neither. That is why there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link here: the practice it would switch off does not happen.
  • To limit use of sensitive personal information — we collect none as the statutes define it. No Social Security number, no financial account number, no precise geolocation, no biometric, genetic or health data.
  • Not to be discriminated against — using a right will never change your price, your service, or whether we accept your order.
  • To appeal — if we refuse a request we explain why and how to appeal, and an owner reviews it.

Californians hold these rights under the CCPA as the CPRA amended it. Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and the other states that have passed comprehensive privacy statutes hold rights that are broadly the same.

Making a request

Email howdy@sundaydrivegoods.com with Privacy request in the subject line, or telephone (520) 555-0183 during Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm Mountain Standard Time. Note that Arizona does not observe daylight saving, so from March to November we are an hour behind the rest of Mountain Time.

Say which right you are exercising and give the email address or order number you used so that we can find your records. We acknowledge within 10 days and answer substantively within 45 days. If we genuinely need longer we tell you inside those 45 days and take up to a further 45, never more than 90 days in total. An authorised agent will be asked for written authority, and we will verify with you directly.

Requests are free. A manifestly unfounded or excessive one could attract a reasonable fee, but we would explain and agree it with you first.

How long we keep it

Order records :: 7 years, as tax and accounting rules require. Payment records (last four digits only) :: 7 years, with the order. Messages and email :: 24 months from the last message, then deleted. Dispatch list address :: Until you unsubscribe, then removed within 10 days. Server logs :: 30 days. Returns and refunds :: 7 years, with the order.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and us is encrypted. Card details are entered on the processor's own PCI-DSS compliant page and never reach our systems. Order records are accessible only to the two owners, each with a separate account and multi-factor authentication. Paper records are locked.

No system is perfectly secure and we are not going to claim otherwise. If a breach affected your personal information we would notify you and the relevant state authorities without undue delay, as state breach-notification law requires.

Children

This is a general-audience household goods shop. It is not directed to children under 13, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and nothing here is designed to appeal particularly to children.

Some products are unsuitable for young children — small domino tiles and draughts, and a record player with a delicate stylus — and the relevant pages say so. If we learn we hold information from a child under 13 we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has given us information, email howdy@sundaydrivegoods.com and we will remove it and write back to confirm.

Orders can only be placed by people aged 18 or over; the condition is written into the terms of service.

Changes

The in-force date at the top always shows the current version. For any change that materially affects your rights we will email the dispatch list and put a notice on the home page for 30 days, and we will not apply a materially worse policy retroactively to information already held.
