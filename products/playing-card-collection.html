The detail

About this one

Three decks, because two get you a game of canasta and the third is the one that goes in the car. All poker size, 2.5 by 3.5 inches, on 310gsm card stock with an embossed linen finish and a plastic coating.

The linen finish is the part worth paying for. It is a fine crosshatch pressed into the surface that stops cards sticking to each other and lets a deck shuffle properly rather than sliding in a block. A plain smooth card stock feels fine on day one and is unusable after a month.

**One of the three decks is printed with large indexes** — the corner numbers are 0.75 inches tall instead of the standard 0.5 — which is simply easier to read from across a table, in a dim room, or in a fan. The other two are standard index. All three have the same back design, so a marked or lost card from one is obvious.

Each deck has 52 cards plus two jokers and comes in its own tuck box.

    • Keep them dry. Card stock warps permanently once it gets damp.
    • Do not wash a deck or wipe it with anything wet; a dry cloth is all it wants.
