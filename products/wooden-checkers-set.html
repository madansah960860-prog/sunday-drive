The detail

About this one

Checkers is the game everybody already knows the rules to, which is why it is the one that actually gets played. This is the grown-up version of it: a solid wood board rather than card, and turned hardwood pieces rather than plastic counters.

The board is 14 inches square with 1.5-inch squares in natural and dark-stained hardwood, finished with a matt lacquer. It is 0.6 inches thick and sits flat on a table without sliding, which a folding card board never quite does.

Twenty-four draughts, 1.2 inches across and 0.35 thick, twelve natural and twelve stained, each with a milled rim so a stack of two stays put when you king one. They live in a drawstring cotton bag.

The board has no storage inside it, which is the trade for being one solid piece. If you want board and pieces in a single case, the chess set folds and this does not.

  • Looking after it

    • Wipe with a dry or barely damp cloth. No polish and no solvent on the lacquer.
    • Keep out of direct sunlight; the natural and stained woods fade at different rates.
    • Do not stand the board on its edge for long periods; solid wood can cup.
    • Store the draughts in the bag rather than loose in a drawer.
