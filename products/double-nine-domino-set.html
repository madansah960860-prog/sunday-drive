The detail

About this one

Double-nine is the set to own if more than four people ever play. A double-six set has 28 tiles and runs out of hand at five players; double-nine has 55 and takes six comfortably. It is also the set Mexican Train is played with, which is the reason most people go looking for one.

The tiles are 2 by 1 inch and 0.4 inches thick — noticeably chunkier than a travel set — with a spinner pin in the centre of each and colour-coded pips so you can read a nine across a table without counting. They are urea resin, which is the material proper dominoes have been made from since the 1930s: it has weight, it clicks rather than clatters, and it does not chip at the corners the way injection-moulded polystyrene does.

The box is solid hardwood with a sliding lid, 9 by 5 by 2 inches, and every tile has its own slot so nothing rattles around in transit.

Rules for Mexican Train, Chicken Foot, Block and Draw are printed on a folded card inside.
