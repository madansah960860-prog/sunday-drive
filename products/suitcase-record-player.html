The detail

About this one

A suitcase player is not a hi-fi and we are not going to pretend it is. What it is, is a record player you can carry into another room by its handle, put on a table, and have playing forty seconds later with nothing else plugged into it.

It plays all three speeds — 33⅓, 45 and 78 rpm — from a belt-driven platter, which is quieter than the direct-drive motors these usually get. The cartridge is a standard ceramic type with a replaceable sapphire stylus; a spare stylus is in the box, and replacements are a common part. There is a 45 rpm adapter clipped inside the lid so it does not get lost.

The two speakers are 2.5-inch drivers in the base, 3 watts a side. They are perfectly fine for a kitchen and modest for a living room, which is why there is also a 3.5mm line output on the back to feed a proper amplifier, plus a headphone socket.

The case is a light blue leatherette over ply, with two latches and a carry handle. Closed it is 14 by 10 by 5 inches and weighs 5.6 pounds. A ceramic cartridge tracks heavier than a magnetic one, which is worth knowing: it is not the machine to play an irreplaceable record on.

  • Looking after it

    • Change the stylus roughly every 300 hours of playing. A worn stylus damages records.
    • Keep the lid closed when it is not in use; dust on the platter ends up in the groove.
    • Wipe the leatherette with a barely damp cloth. No solvents.
    • Do not stack records on the platter or leave one sitting under the arm for weeks.
    • Not suitable for rare or irreplaceable records — a ceramic cartridge tracks heavy.
