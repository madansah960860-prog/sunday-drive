The detail

About this one

A gallery wall goes wrong when the frames do not match. Six matching frames in three sizes solves that in one purchase, and the three sizes are what stops a matching set looking like a filing system.

You get two 8 by 10 inch, two 5 by 7, and two 4 by 6. Every frame is solid pine with a matt black painted finish and a 0.8-inch face, with real glass rather than acrylic — glass is heavier and it can break, but it does not scratch when you dust it and it does not yellow.

Each frame comes with a white bevel-cut mat: the 8 by 10 mats to 5 by 7, the 5 by 7 mats to 4 by 6, and the 4 by 6 mats to 3.5 by 5. Take the mat out and the full size is available. The backs have both a sawtooth hanger and a fold-out easel, so every frame works on a wall or on a shelf, in portrait or landscape.

There is a paper hanging template in the box — a sheet you tape to the wall, mark through, and remove — which is the difference between an even gallery wall and eleven trial holes.
