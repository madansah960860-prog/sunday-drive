The detail

About this one

Five frames in a weathered grey-brown finish, sized the same but finished differently from one another — a barn-wood look, so they read as a collected group rather than a matching set. If the black set is the tidy option, this is the other one.

All five take a 5 by 7 inch photograph, or 4 by 6 with the white mat in place. The mouldings are pine with a distressed painted finish, 1.1 inches across the face, which is chunkier than the black set and part of the look.

The glazing is styrene rather than glass. That is a deliberate choice on a distressed frame: it is lighter, it will not break, and these are the frames most likely to end up on a stair wall or handed to a grandchild. It does scratch more easily than glass, so dust it rather than wiping it dry.

Each has a sawtooth hanger and a fold-out easel, and works portrait or landscape.
