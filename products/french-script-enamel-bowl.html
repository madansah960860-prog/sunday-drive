The detail

About this one

A bowl in the old French kitchen pattern: cream body, a navy rolled rim, and "Ma Cuisine" — my kitchen — lettered on the side. Ours is newly made to that pattern; it is not an antique and we do not sell it as one.

Ten inches across the rim and 4.5 deep, holding 3 quarts, which is the size that actually gets used: big enough for a double batch of dough, small enough to hold against your hip while you stir. The rolled rim is what makes it pourable and what makes it comfortable to grip with a wet hand.

Enamel is a genuinely good mixing surface. It is non-porous so it does not take up smells the way plastic does, it does not react with acid the way bare aluminium does, and it goes straight from the counter into the refrigerator without covering anything.

Dishwasher safe and oven safe to 500°F. Not microwave safe, because it is steel. It chips if dropped, and a chip to bare steel will rust.
